# Reference: frame (floor / first futtock) to keel connection, from Ships of Scale forum
Thread "Princess Charlotte 1814" (shipsofscale.com/sosforums/threads/princess-charlotte-1814.10860/), posts 277585, 277612, 277829, 277843 (wreck survey CAD by the poster, not Royal Caroline).
- Floors sit in a NOTCH cut into the top of the keel; the floor bottom is flat, no notch in the floor (post 277829, 277585 "the top surface of the keel was notched to fit the floors").
- The first futtocks (the other layer of the frame) are notched OVER the keel (the "ח"), which stops sideways movement; the floor in the keel notch stops movement fore and aft (post 277843).
- A cross chock is bolted to the floors and a rod runs through keelson, chock and keel (post 277829).
Images (CAD views, same thread): pc_277585_0 (survey drawing), pc_277612_0/1 (floors V sitting in the keel notches), pc_277829_0..2 (V floor with its stub in a keel notch, first futtock notched over the keel).
